Free Organogram Builder — make organizational charts online

Organogram Builder is a free, open-source organizational chart maker that runs entirely in your browser. Build an organogram by hand or import it from an Excel or CSV file, switch between top-down, horizontal and inverted layouts, add people and photos, and export to PDF, Excel, CSV or PowerPoint. No sign-up and no servers — your data stays on your device.

What you can do

  • Create an org chart online for free — no account, no download, no watermark.
  • Import an organogram from an Excel spreadsheet or CSV with Name and Parent columns.
  • Export your organizational chart to PDF, Excel, CSV and PowerPoint.
  • Choose top-down, left-to-right (horizontal) or inverted (bottom-up) layouts.
  • Add employee photos and titles for a people-style org chart.
  • Start from templates for a company, corporate, non-profit, hospital, HR or school organogram.

How to make an organogram

  1. Open the free organogram builder in your browser.
  2. Pick a starter template or import your team from Excel or CSV.
  3. Add, rename and drag boxes to match your company structure.
  4. Export the finished org chart to PDF, PowerPoint, Excel or CSV.

Frequently asked questions

Is this organogram builder free?

Yes. It is completely free and open-source under the MIT license, with no sign-up, no account and no watermark.

Can I make an org chart from an Excel spreadsheet?

Yes. Import an .xlsx or .csv file with Name and Parent columns and the organization chart is generated automatically.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays in your own browser. There is no server and nothing is uploaded. You can also save to a local database file on your computer.

Can I export the organogram?

Yes. You can export to PDF, Excel, CSV and a ready-to-present PowerPoint deck.
